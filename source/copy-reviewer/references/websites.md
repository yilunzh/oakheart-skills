# Websites and interfaces

Establish the page's job and the visitor's likely knowledge. Distinguish acquisition, comparison, booking and in-product task completion. Inspect headings, supporting text, labels, states and consequential links together when available.

For marketing pages, make the offer and its supported value understandable, arrange detail around the visitor's questions, and give actions meaningful labels. Review scanning and hierarchy without reducing every section to slogans or forcing one CTA everywhere. Keep eligibility, price and consequential conditions near the decision. Do not claim absent capabilities without inspecting the incumbent or receiving reliable evidence.

For interfaces, prefer consistent terms and precise states over verbal variety. Distinguish draft, submitted, pending, confirmed and failed; label what the action actually does. Error copy should explain a supported recovery action. Do not imply success, storage, refunds or guarantees the behavior does not establish.

Check mobile wrapping, truncation, link context and accessibility only where rendered evidence exists; otherwise flag them for visual review. Content review alone cannot establish conversion or usability gains.
